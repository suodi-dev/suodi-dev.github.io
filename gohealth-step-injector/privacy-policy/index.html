Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

App: GoHealth Step Injector

This privacy policy explains how GoHealth Step Injector ("the App") handles information. We are committed to transparency and to protecting your privacy.

Data We Do NOT Collect

This App generates manual, user-defined simulation data locally on-device and writes it to Android Health Connect. It does not:

  • Read, collect, or retrieve any existing health or fitness data from Health Connect (the App uses write-only permissions).
  • Collect, store, or transmit any personal or health tracking data to external servers.
  • Share any data with advertising networks, analytics providers, or any third party.

How the App Works

  1. You type a desired step count into the App.
  2. The App generates a synthetic step/distance record locally on your device.
  3. The App writes that record one-way into Android Health Connect.

All processing happens entirely on your device. No data leaves your device.

Advertising

The App may display rewarded video ads to unlock larger step amounts. Advertisements are served by a third-party ad SDK. Because the App never reads your real health data, the ad SDK is only shown a user-typed step count — never any actual health or fitness information. No health data is shared with any ad network.

Health Connect Permissions

The App requests only the following write permissions from Health Connect:

  • android.permission.health.WRITE_STEPS
  • android.permission.health.WRITE_DISTANCE

The App requests no read permissions.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
